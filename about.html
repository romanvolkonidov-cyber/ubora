Our Story

A decade of Ubora — Swahili for Excellence.

Ubora Services Limited is a duly registered Kenyan company that has been delivering integrated Facility Hygiene and Technical services since 2014. We exist to make workplaces, hospitals and homes safer, cleaner and more productive.

Who we are

Built on integrity. Driven by detail. Trusted by Kenya's best.

We were founded with a simple promise: deliver work so consistently good that customers never need to look elsewhere. From our roots in Nairobi and Kisumu, we have grown into a multi-disciplinary team serving hospitals, universities, energy companies, manufacturers and homes nationwide.

Whether the brief is daily cleaning across a busy hospital, a one-off fumigation, or wiring a new commercial block — every contract is treated with the same standard of care, accountability and professional rigour.
